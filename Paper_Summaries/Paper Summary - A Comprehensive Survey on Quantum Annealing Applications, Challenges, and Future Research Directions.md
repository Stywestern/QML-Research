Concept: Physical Realization of Quantum Annealing & Tunneling
* **Core Idea:** In Quantum Annealing, the "loss landscape" is not a software metaphor. The mathematical problem is physically manufactured onto the chip as a magnetic energy landscape. 
1. Qubits as Physical Magnets 
	* Qubits are microscopic loops of superconducting wire. 
	* **Clockwise Current:** Generates an **UP** magnetic field (`1`). 
	* **Counter-Clockwise Current:** Generates a **DOWN** magnetic field (`0`). 
2. $H_{\text{final}}$: Encoding the Math Problem 
	* Mathematical constraints are translated into physical **magnetic couplings** between qubit loops. 
	* If two variables are constrained to be opposite, their qubits are magnetically coupled to repel each other. 
	* The "lowest loss" solution to the math problem corresponds directly to the **lowest physical magnetic energy state** of the chip. 
3. $H_{\text{initial}}$: The Transverse Magnetic Field 
	* At the start ($s = 0$), a strong sideways (transverse) magnetic field is applied across all qubits. 
	* This overpowers the problem couplings and forces all qubits to point **SIDEWAYS** (a physical state of equal superposition between UP and DOWN). 
4. The Annealing Schedule $s(t)$ $$H(t) = (1 - s(t))H_{\text{initial}} + s(t)H_{\text{final}}$$
	* As time $s$ progresses from $0$ to $1$: 
	* $H_{\text{initial}}$ (sideways field) is gradually turned off. 
	* $H_{\text{final}}$ (problem couplings) is gradually turned on. 
5. Quantum Tunneling vs. Classical Climbing 
	* **Classical Optimization:** Requires "thermal energy" or momentum to climb over energy barriers to escape shallow "local minima" valleys. 
	* **Quantum Tunneling:** As the transverse field decays, the quantum wavefunction allows the magnetic states of the qubits to **tunnel directly through energy barriers** without needing to climb over them, rapidly settling into the true global minimum.
